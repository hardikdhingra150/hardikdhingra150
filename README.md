## 05 / You're on the pit wall

**A tiny race strategy game. Two calls. One finish.**

You're the race engineer in this fictional scenario. Pick **one option per round**, open it to hear team radio, and keep your points.

Maximum score: **4**. Play directly in this README.

### Lap 38 / The sky changes

You're on worn slicks. Rain is increasing, the circuit is wet without standing water, and more rain is expected.

A pit stop costs time, but the current tyres are losing grip. What's your call?

<details>
<summary><strong>A · Stay out on slicks</strong></summary>

**RADIO:** “We're sliding through every braking zone.”  
**+0 points.** You saved the stop, but the conditions no longer suit these tyres.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>B · Box for intermediates</strong></summary>

**RADIO:** “Grip is coming back. We can work with this.”  
**+2 points.** In this scenario, intermediates suit the wet surface without standing water.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>C · Box for full wets</strong></summary>

**RADIO:** “Stable, but we're giving away pace.”  
**+1 point.** You've planned for heavier water than this scenario currently has.

</details>

### Lap 49 / Bring it home

Assume the car is now on intermediates. You're ahead, the track remains wet, and your engineer reports that tyre wear is manageable.

The finish is two laps away. What's your call?

<details>
<summary><strong>A · Push beyond the grip limit</strong></summary>

**RADIO:** “That was a big moment. Keep it on the circuit.”  
**+0 points.** You took unnecessary risk while already leading.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>B · Pit again without a tyre problem</strong></summary>

**RADIO:** “We've handed away the position.”  
**+1 point.** A fresh set is tempting, but the scenario gives no reason to pay for another stop.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>C · Manage the tyres and protect the lead</strong></summary>

**RADIO:** “Smooth inputs. Two clean laps. Bring it home.”  
**+2 points.** You've matched the risk to the position and remaining distance.

</details>
